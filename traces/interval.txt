// CLEARED FOR TRANSIT

You wait. It stops - you feel the stop more than see it, the cold settling, the shadow fixing - and you mark it and you go, across the gap between one of its arrivals and the next, not breathing, your legs moving on a count your mind is too loud to hear. The blister hatch slams behind your heel. You seal it by hand.

The comms board takes your distress ping on the first try. Rescue is hours out. You watch the sealed hatch and you do not look away from it, because you have learned at least that much.

Behind it, in the hold, eleven cases sit where they sit and one keeps its slow appointment with a berth you are no longer in. On the board the manifest holds its last line, patient. Released: 1 of 12. Pending.
