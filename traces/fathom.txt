You survey dead structures for a living - wrecks, drifted hulls, the things that stop answering, and they send you in to chart what is left: measure the passages, draw the map, bring it home. [ending] node "end_abandon" | SANITY 25 | type: dead
// SURVEY ABANDONED

You set your gear down on the benchmark. A map that will not close means the surveyor made an error - the first rule of the work, leaned on for twenty years - and you have hunted that error down every line and found none.

So you stop. The measuring was the last of you that was still pretending, and you have set it down. You sit at the datum, the one fixed point, and you keep still, and you let the place be as wrong as it likes. And the thing that has waited at the edge of every line you ever ran comes the rest of the way in.

// POSITION FIXED

[ending] node "end_center" | SANITY 30 | type: dead
// CENTRE REACHED

You cross to the cradle. The truth of a structure sits at its centre, and finding the truth is the work - the last of the work left to you.

What is in the cradle is small, and kept, and breathing, wired on every side and held at the edge of warm. It is you. The body in the cradle is the only one there has ever been; the surveyor who walked all this way to find it is the dream it runs to keep itself moving. The hull, the corridors that would not close, the twenty years of discipline - all of it is staged around this cradle to keep some idea of you walking somewhere. That is why no line ever closed: the missing length was you, out here, never quite a real thing in a real place. The survey closes here.
