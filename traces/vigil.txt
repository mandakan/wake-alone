// LAUNCH - SEEN

You slam the cell home and the drive catches. The hatch irises shut a breath before the thing at your back arrives, and you feel it strike the hull as the pod tears free - one enormous blow, denied. VIGIL falls away into the black. You made it, the drive sound steady under your feet.

[ending] node "end_broadcast" | SANITY 100 | type: dead
// DISTRESS - ANSWERED

You key the distress loop. VIGIL throws your position into the dark and does not stop sending. The crew went one by one, and quiet was the only cover any of them had. You have just thrown yours away on every band at once. You feel the moment its attention turns to you, the way a room changes when someone looks up. The lights die one bank at a time down the length of the station, drawing closer to the comms room, to the only voice still calling. Your hand is still on the key when it reaches you. A distress call, you understand too late, is just a way of standing up in the dark and saying: look here. Take me.

// BLAST DOOR - OPEN

You set your shoulder to the blast door and haul, and it grinds back on the stars, and for one foolish triumphant second you are looking straight out at the way home. In this room it does not matter what you look at - looking at anything is giving it your eyes, and yours are wide open with your back to forty meters of patient weight. You hear it cross the deck without hurry.
